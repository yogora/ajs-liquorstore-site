About AJ's

Carterville's neighborhood liquor store since 2011.

AJ's opened in 2011 as Carterville's only retail liquor store — local ordinances don't allow gas stations or other shops in town to sell alcohol, so we've been the one stop here from the start.

Our family has been in the alcohol business for over 30 years, having run a beer distributorship before opening the store. That background shows up in how we keep things — organized, clean, and stocked with the name brands you're used to.

We're a small, local business, and we like it that way. Stop in, say hello, and you'll probably run into someone you know.

Food Trucks

Every Friday, we host local food trucks in our parking lot — grab dinner along with your beer run and make a night of it.

Drive-Thru

In more of a hurry? Our drive-thru window makes for quick beer, wine, and liquor pickup without having to leave your car.

What to Expect at a Local Liquor Store

A good neighborhood liquor store carries more than a few dusty bottles on a shelf. Look for cold beer stocked daily, a wine selection that covers everything from an easy weeknight red to a bottle worth saving for a celebration, and a liquor and spirits aisle deep enough to cover top-shelf bourbon, well-known vodkas, and the mixers to go with them. Ready-to-drink cocktails and seltzers round things out for anyone who wants something simple to grab on the way out the door.

  • Beer — domestic, imported, and craft, always kept cold.
  • Wine — reds, whites, and everything in between, for any occasion.
  • Liquor & Spirits — top-shelf and everyday favorites across every category.
  • Ready-to-Drink — canned cocktails and seltzers for a grab-and-go option.
