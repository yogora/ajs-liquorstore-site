About AJ's

Carterville's neighborhood liquor store since 2011.

AJ's has served Carterville as a locally owned liquor store since 2011. We're less than a quarter mile off Highway 13, an easy stop on the daily commute or any time you're passing through.

We keep the store organized and stocked with the beer, wine, and spirits brands you already know, with a drive-thru window for quick pickup when you're short on time.

We're a small, local business focused on quality, service, and value for our customers.

Food Trucks

Every Friday, we host local food trucks in our parking lot. Everyone's welcome, whether you're stopping in or just passing through.
